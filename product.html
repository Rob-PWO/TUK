The Fox Frontier II XL Bivvy features an upgraded and improved design with a number of alterations, additions and extras over the original.

Constructed from 100% Polyester Ventec fabric with a blackout laminate lining and a 20,000mm hydrostatic head, the Frontier II shrugs off the worst of the British weather. A lightweight aluminium frame with high, flat-back design gives maximum headroom and usable internal space, while a new geared ETS truss prevents frame collapse during setup.

Supplied with a solid infill panel plus three door options - solid, clear, and camo mozzy mesh - and four internal mesh pockets for safe storage, the Frontier II is built for comfort on long sessions. New spring-loaded hinged, moulded and keyed elbow joints ensure fast setup and perfect alignment every time.

Key features

  • 100% Polyester Ventec fabric with blackout laminate lining
  • 20,000mm hydrostatic head
  • Lightweight aluminium frame, high flat-back design
  • Geared ETS truss prevents frame collapse
  • Three door options: solid, clear & camo mozzy mesh
  • Four internal mesh pockets for safe storage
  • Spring-loaded hinged, moulded & keyed elbow joints
  • Vapour Peak included as standard
