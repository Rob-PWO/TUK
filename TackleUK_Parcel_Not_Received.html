⚠ Important Notice: Please Read Before Continuing

Parcel disputes are treated as formal fraud investigations. All claims are cross-referenced against carrier GPS data, proof of delivery records, route manifests, and doorstep camera footage. Both DHL and Royal Mail retain detailed records of every scan event, GPS waypoint, and delivery attempt.

In some cases an investigator may attend your delivery address. You will be asked to sign a statutory legal affidavit. Making a false statement is a criminal offence under the Perjury Act 1911 and the Fraud Act 2006, carrying up to seven years’ imprisonment.

Fraudulent claims are referred to our legal team and, where appropriate, to Action Fraud and local trading standards.
